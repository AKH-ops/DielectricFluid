DielectricFluid.com is an independent, vendor-neutral register of dielectric fluids — the liquids that cool data centres, semiconductor tools, transformers and electronics by touching live components. We publish what each maker states about its fluid, with every value linked to the page of the document it came from. When a reader is ready to buy, our [desk](/desk/) helps them find supply.

## Who runs it

DielectricFluid.com is founded and edited by **Ahmad Hassan, PhD**. With a background in research and engineering, he runs the site the way he would run any technical review: primary documents first, every number traceable, uncertainty stated plainly. He is accountable for everything published here.

We are not a fluid maker, distributor or system vendor, and no maker has paid for or reviewed anything on this site.

## What we do

- **The register:** typical properties of dielectric fluids, cited to the maker's datasheet page, downloadable under CC BY 4.0. [Open it](/fluids/).
- **Tools:** the [Novec & Fluorinert replacement finder](/novec-replacement/) and the [PFAS tracker](/pfas/).
- **Guides:** the fundamentals, written from the same cited data. [All guides](/guides/).
- **The desk:** tell us what you are cooling, and we shortlist fluids and go to the makers for you. [Ask the desk](/desk/).

## How we are paid

The site is free to read and carries no display advertising. When the desk introduces a buyer to a supplier, we may receive a referral fee from that supplier, and we tell the buyer before they decide. **No one can pay to change a value, a ranking or a recommendation.** Makers may in future pay us to keep their records complete and current. That never buys placement, and it will be disclosed on the record. See our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
