**In plain words.** We compare dielectric fluids using the makers' own published values, and we help buyers reach suppliers. We do not test fluids, and we do not vet, endorse or guarantee any supplier, product or specification. Check everything that matters with the supplier and your own engineers before relying on it.

## 1. Comparative information only

The register and guides give general information about dielectric fluids. Fluid selection, system design, material compatibility, safety and regulatory compliance depend on facts a website cannot assess. Nothing here is engineering, safety, legal or financial advice.

## 2. Typical values, as published

Values are the makers' typical figures, recorded as printed and linked to their documents. They are not specifications. Makers revise documents, and test conditions vary between makers. The certificate of analysis that ships with a batch, and the maker's current datasheet, take precedence over anything here.

## 3. No guarantees

We work to keep the register accurate and current, but we do not guarantee that any page is complete, error-free or up to date. Product availability and regulatory status change, the PFAS landscape especially.
