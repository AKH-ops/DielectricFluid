## Sources we accept

Every value in the register comes from the **maker's own document**: a technical datasheet, product information sheet or brochure. When a maker has withdrawn a document, we use an archived copy of that same document (for example from the Internet Archive) and say so. We do not take values from resellers' summaries, marketplaces or search snippets. Regulatory and market statements cite the regulator, the company's own filing or announcement, or named reputable press.

## How values are recorded

- **As printed.** We record the number, qualifier ("> 60", "≈ 330") and condition (temperature, method, gap) the maker prints. We do not round, average or derive values.
- **One unit per column.** Where a maker prints another unit (kg/m³, J/kg·K, cal/g, torr), we convert it and keep the printed figure beside it.
- **Cited to the page.** Each value links to the document and page it came from, with the date we checked it.
- **Typical, not specification.** Makers publish typical values. The certificate of analysis that ships with a batch is the binding document.

## When a product gets its own page

A product line gets its own page only when every grade carries density, viscosity, thermal conductivity, specific heat, dielectric strength, and a boiling or flash point, all cited. Grades from one datasheet share one page; we do not make near-identical pages per grade. Incomplete records stay in the register table, marked as such.

## PFAS status

We classify PFAS status only from evidence the maker publishes: its own statement, or a disclosed structure tested against the OECD (2021) definition. Where the structure is not disclosed, the status is "undetermined". The evidence for every fluid is quoted on the [PFAS tracker](/pfas/).

## How we use AI

We use AI tools, under the editor's supervision, to find documents, extract values and draft text. Nothing is published unless every value has been checked against the maker's document and linked to it, so you can check it too. The editor, Ahmad Hassan, PhD, is accountable for everything published.

## Money and independence

When the desk introduces a buyer to a supplier, we may receive a referral fee from that supplier, and we tell the buyer before they decide. Fees never affect which fluids appear, how they rank, or what we write about them. No maker reviews our pages before publication. We run no display ads.

## Corrections

If a value is wrong, or a maker has revised a sheet, email [hello@dielectricfluid.com](mailto:hello@dielectricfluid.com) with the page and, ideally, the source. We correct verified errors and log every change, with its date, on the [updates page](/updates/).
