Every change to a value, a record or our method is logged here with its date. To report an error, email [hello@dielectricfluid.com](mailto:hello@dielectricfluid.com) with the page and the source.

## 2026-10-11 — the register launches

- The site moves from its earlier guide format to a **cited register**. Every value now links to the page of the maker's document it came from.
- **First records (21 grades, 9 product lines, 5 makers):** Engineered Fluids ElectroCool EC-110, EC-120, EC-122, EC-130 and EC-140; Syensqo Galden HT55 to HT270 (nine grades); Cargill NatureCool 2000 and Envirotemp FR3; Chemours Opteon 2P50 (developmental); and 3M Novec 7000, Novec 7100, Fluorinert FC-72 and FC-770 (discontinued). The 3M sheets are archived copies where 3M has withdrawn the originals.
- **Incomplete records:** Envirotemp FR3 and Novec 7100 are in the table, but their full property sets are still being verified, so they have no pages yet.
- **New tools:** the [Novec & Fluorinert replacement finder](/novec-replacement/) and the [PFAS tracker](/pfas/).
- **Guides rewritten** with every claim cited. Earlier unsourced statements, including cost comparisons and market claims, were removed rather than carried over.
- **Old addresses** (`/p/…html`) now redirect permanently to their new pages.
