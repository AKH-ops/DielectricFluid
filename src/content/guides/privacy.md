**The short version.** You can read every page without giving us anything. We set no cookies and run no advertising or analytics trackers. If you send a request to the desk, we use what you send to answer it. We share it with suppliers only if you agree. Questions: [hello@dielectricfluid.com](mailto:hello@dielectricfluid.com).

## 1. What we collect

- **What you send us.** When you use the desk form or email us, we receive what you provide: typically your name, work email, company, application, the fluid you use or need, volume, timeline and any message. Please do not send confidential specifications or sensitive personal data.
- **Connection data.** Our host, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as IP address, browser type and requested page to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

## 2. Who processes it for us

- **Cloudflare** hosts and serves the site and runs the desk form. Your answers are stored in a database in our own Cloudflare account and emailed to the desk. We store the country Cloudflare reports for the request, not your IP address. No outside form service handles them.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that the form is sent by a person rather than a bot. It runs in your browser when you open the desk page and processes technical data under [Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).
- **Our email provider** receives and stores the messages you send us and the desk's copy of each form request.

## 3. How we use it

To understand and answer your request and, where you ask us to, to obtain quotes or make an introduction to suppliers. Connection data is used only to run and secure the site.

## 4. When we share it

- **With suppliers, only with your agreement.** The desk asks before passing your details to a supplier. You can ask us to answer your questions without contacting anyone, and we will.
- **Referral fees.** If an introduction leads to business, we may receive a referral fee from the supplier. Under some US state privacy laws, sharing details in return for a fee can count as a "sale" or "sharing". You can opt out at any time.
- **Legal reasons:** if the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.

We never sell lists or share your details for advertising.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example under UK and EU GDPR, or US state laws — you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, object to its use, or opt out of sharing. Email [hello@dielectricfluid.com](mailto:hello@dielectricfluid.com). We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. Our lawful basis for answering a request is your request itself, and for introductions it is your consent.

## 6. Retention and security

We keep request information as long as we need to answer it, complete any introduction and keep a record of consent, then delete or anonymise it. We use reasonable safeguards, but no transmission is completely secure.
