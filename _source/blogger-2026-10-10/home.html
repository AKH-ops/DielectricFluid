The core decision

Single-phase or two-phase?

It’s the first fork in any immersion project — and it drives which fluids are even on the table.

  • Single-phase keeps the fluid liquid and pumps it to a heat exchanger. Simpler, fewer failure points, PFAS-free options are widely available.
  • Two-phase lets the fluid boil and condense for extreme heat loads and low PUE, but historically depended on fluorinated fluids hit by the 3M exit and PFAS rules.
  • The consequence: your phase choice and PFAS exposure narrow the fluid shortlist before you ever talk to a supplier.
